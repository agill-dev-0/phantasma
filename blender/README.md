Greek Art Vocabulary:

For the border ring:

Meander (Greek key) — the interlocking rectilinear band. The single most recognizably Greek motif, and ideal for a circular surround because it tiles cleanly. Comes in single, double, and swastika-meander variants; stick to the simple running version.

Egg-and-dart — alternating ovoids and arrowheads, from Ionic capitals. Gives you strong alternating relief, which catches light beautifully and is a great showcase for a PBR surround.

Bead-and-reel — alternating discs and spools. Finer scale, good as a secondary inner ring.

Guilloche — interwoven ribbon bands, often with rosettes in the openings. More ornate, reads as jewelry.

Wave scroll (Vitruvian scroll) — a running wave pattern, softer than meander.

For the field or accents:

Laurel or olive wreath — victory and Apollo respectively. Olive is Athena's, laurel is Delphic and tied directly to the oracle, so laurel is the thematically correct one for you.

Palmette (anthemion) — fan-shaped stylized honeysuckle, usually alternating with lotus. The most common Greek decorative filler.

Acanthus — the leaf from Corinthian capitals, deeply lobed. Excellent relief subject.

Rosette — stylized flower, good for corners or spacing elements.

Thematically loaded, use sparingly:

Omphalos — the navel stone at Delphi, often carved with a net pattern (agrenon). Direct oracle reference and very few people use it.

Tripod — the Pythia's seat, and the emblem of Delphi. Strong, specific, and the single most "oracle" symbol available.

Gorgoneion — the Medusa head, used apotropaically on shields and pediments. Powerful but competes with your skull; probably one face too many.

Ouroboros, caduceus, kantharos, lyre — all usable as small accents.

** Recommendation ** meander on the outer ring, an inscription band inside it in Greek capitals, egg-and-dart as the inner frame, laurel or a tripod as a small accent. That's restrained, unmistakably Greek, and gives your renderer three distinct relief scales to show off.

One note on the inscription: use authentic Greek lettering rather than Latin letters styled to look Greek. Something like ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΕΑΥΤΟΝ (know thyself) or ΜΗΔΕΝ ΑΓΑΝ (nothing in excess) — both were actually inscribed at Delphi, which makes them exactly right for your oracle.
